Sensex, Nifty extend rebound as IT and pharma lift benchmarks

Indian equities closed higher for a second straight session on Friday, with the Sensex adding 308.49 points, or 0.42%, to 74,459.53 and the Nifty 50 rising 116.35 points, or 0.51%, to 23,053.10. Buying in information technology and pharmaceutical shares did most of the work, helped by resilient corporate earnings and the unwinding of the global AI trade, which turned attention toward domestic technology names. Over the week, both benchmarks gained close to 2%.

The advance was narrow. The Nifty MidCap 100 fell 0.93% and the Nifty SmallCap 100 dropped 1.85%, and of 3,633 stocks traded on the NSE, 2,511 declined against 1,013 that advanced. The rupee strengthened 33 paise to close at 94.93 per dollar. Bullion stayed under pressure: 24K gold fell ₹1,364 to ₹1,40,891 per 10 g and 999 silver dropped ₹6,678 to ₹1,83,800 per kg, tracking a global selloff as firm US inflation data kept hopes of an early rate cut in check.

Why this matters to you: A rising headline index and a falling advance-decline count can both be true at once. It means a handful of large, index-heavy stocks are doing the work while most shares slip. Reading market breadth alongside the index level is how you tell whether a rally is broad or narrow.

How to read this page

A daily market page is not a signal to act. The useful habit is to look at three things only: the direction of the index over weeks rather than hours, whether foreign money is buying or selling, and whether anything changed in interest rates or inflation. Everything else is noise dressed up as news. If you want the numbers without the drama, this page is for you — and every figure here is checked before it is published.
