Daily market brief · 12 October 2026

Oil jumped, foreign money kept leaving - and the market gave back Friday's gains

Indian equities erased early gains on Monday and closed lower: the Sensex fell 547.65 points (0.76%) to 71,924.68 and the Nifty 50 dropped 157.10 points (0.70%) to 22,363.35. A jump in Brent crude to about $105.8 a barrel on fresh Middle East tensions, persistent foreign outflows and bond yields above 5.2% all weighed on sentiment, with banking, FMCG and IT shares leading the fall.

The rupee was the exception. It rose nearly 1% to about 95.77 to the dollar after the RBI moved to curb dollar demand - including selling dollars directly to oil marketing companies. Gold held roughly steady, near Rs 1.50 lakh per 10 grams.

Why this matters to you: a falling market and a recovering rupee pull in opposite directions. The weak rupee had been squeezing importers and oil bills, so its rebound eases one pressure - even as equity investors stay nervous about oil and foreign selling.

How to read this page

A daily market page is not a signal to act. The useful habit is to look at three things only: the direction of the index over weeks rather than hours, whether foreign money is buying or selling, and whether anything changed in interest rates or inflation. Everything else is noise dressed up as news. If you want the numbers without the drama, this page is for you — and every figure here is checked before it is published.
