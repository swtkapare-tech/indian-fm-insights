Daily market brief · 28 October 2026

IT and oil drag Sensex down 353 points, Nifty ends near 22,700

Indian equities closed lower on Wednesday, 28 October, as selling in information technology and oil and gas shares outweighed gains in autos, banks and pharma. The Sensex finished 352.64 points, or 0.48%, lower at 73,246.03, recovering most of a sharp morning slide from an intraday low near 72,604. The Nifty 50 settled at 22,698.85, down 50.90 points or 0.22%, after touching 22,497.70 earlier in the day.

Much of the pressure came from outside India. Technology stocks tracked weakness in global peers, while Brent crude near $115 a barrel, after an attack on a refinery in Saudi Arabia, kept oil importers and the currency under watch. Broader indices outperformed the benchmarks, with the midcap and smallcap gauges closing higher, and Jio Platforms' market debut was the session's standout.

Why this matters to you: Index moves and gold prices often respond to different forces. Today the Sensex fell on global tech weakness and expensive crude, while 24K gold edged higher in the domestic benchmark — a reminder that different assets in a portfolio can pull in different directions at the same time.

How to read this page

A daily market page is not a signal to act. The useful habit is to look at three things only: the direction of the index over weeks rather than hours, whether foreign money is buying or selling, and whether anything changed in interest rates or inflation. Everything else is noise dressed up as news. If you want the numbers without the drama, this page is for you — and every figure here is checked before it is published.
