Sensex jumps 414 points, Nifty ends at 23,146 on IT rally

Indian benchmarks rose for a second straight session on Friday. The BSE Sensex climbed 413.66 points, or 0.56%, to close at 74,729.27, while the Nifty 50 added 148.80 points, or 0.65%, to end at 23,146.15. Information-technology shares did most of the heavy lifting after Tech Mahindra reported a strong quarter: Tech Mahindra rose 7.96%, TCS 4.82% and Infosys 4.61%. The rupee firmed to 94.92 against the US dollar, its sixth straight session of gains.

The advance was not broad. The Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 ended marginally lower, and Reliance Industries, Asian Paints and IndiGo fell. For the week, the Sensex rose 3.11% and the Nifty 2.77%. On the bullion side, the India-wide benchmark for 24K gold edged up to Rs 1,48,760 per 10 g, and 999 silver rose to Rs 2,16,462 per kg.

Why this matters to you: A handful of large, heavily weighted companies can move the headline index even when most shares go the other way. That is why the Nifty or Sensex on your screen can rise while the average stock - and your own portfolio - falls on the same day. Knowing which sectors are driving the move tells you more than the index number alone.

How to read this page

A daily market page is not a signal to act. The useful habit is to look at three things only: the direction of the index over weeks rather than hours, whether foreign money is buying or selling, and whether anything changed in interest rates or inflation. Everything else is noise dressed up as news. If you want the numbers without the drama, this page is for you — and every figure here is checked before it is published.
