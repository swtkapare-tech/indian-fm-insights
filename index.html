Market brief · 25 Nov 2026

Sensex rebounds 1,502.29 points to 83,938.28 and the Nifty 50 gains 469.60 to 25,819.25 as foreign inflows return and the rupee jumps to an eight-month high

Banks, financials and FMCG shares led a broad recovery from Monday's sell-off, helped by lower crude prices and strong foreign fund inflows. The rupee closed at 92.23 per dollar, and IBJA's morning benchmark put 24K gold at ₹1,21,310 per 10 g and silver at ₹1,43,522 per kg.
