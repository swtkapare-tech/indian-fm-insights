Market brief · 27 Nov 2026

Sensex slides 989.33 points to 83,339.26 and the Nifty 50 gives up 277.30 to 25,607.20 as information technology, auto and realty shares fall and crude oil above $98 a barrel weighs on sentiment

The Sensex closed 1.17% lower and the Nifty 50 1.07% lower on Friday 27 November 2026, ending a two-day run of gains, while the rupee weakened to 92.98 per dollar and the IBJA 24K gold benchmark rose to ₹1,25,316 per 10 g. The exchanges are shut today, Saturday 28 November 2026, so these are Friday's closing levels, re-checked this afternoon and labelled with the session date.
